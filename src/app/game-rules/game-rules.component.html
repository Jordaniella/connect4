RULES:

  • Objective:
    • The objective of Connect Four is to be the first player to form a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line of four of one's own discs while playing against an AI opponent.
  • Setup:
    • The game is played on a vertical board with 7 columns and 6 rows.
    • Each player, including the AI, chooses a color and is given 21 discs of that color.
    • The board starts empty.
  • Gameplay:
    • You and the AI take turns dropping one of your discs from the top into any of the seven columns.
    • The disc will fall straight down to the lowest available space within the column.
    • You win by connecting four of your discs consecutively in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line before the AI does.
    • If the board fills up before either player achieves four in a row, the game is a draw.
  • Winning the Game:
    • The first player to connect four discs in a row (horizontally, vertically, or diagonally) wins the game.
    • If all 42 spaces are filled without a player connecting four in a row, the game ends in a draw.
  • Strategy Tips:
    • Stay one step ahead of the AI by predicting its moves and blocking its attempts to connect four.
    • Create multiple opportunities for connecting four discs in different directions, making it harder for the AI to block all your moves.
    • Controlling the center of the board can give you an advantage, as it offers more opportunities to connect four.
